# Assessment Rubric — Reflection Assignment

For grading the written reflection (800–1000 words: Experience / Analysis / Learning). Four competencies from established sustainability-competence frameworks, three levels each; weight equally unless your course dictates otherwise.

| Competency | Developing | Proficient | Excellent |
|------------|------------|------------|-----------|
| **Systems thinking** | Describes single events in isolation | Connects stakeholders, constraints and outcomes into cause-effect chains | Analyzes the negotiation as a system: feedback loops, trade-offs, unintended consequences |
| **Collaborative competence** | Reports own group's actions only | Analyzes coalition dynamics including other groups' perspectives | Reflects on trust, communication and what made cooperation succeed or fail — anchored in concrete moments |
| **Normative competence** | States opinions without criteria | Weighs competing values (jobs vs. climate vs. cost vs. reliability) with explicit criteria | Applies justice questions (fair distribution, who pays, who decides) to the outcome and defends a position |
| **Critical thinking** | Retells events | Questions assumptions — own role's and other groups' claims | Evaluates evidence quality (contested numbers, interested sources) and reflects on own biases in role |

**Mapping to the assignment parts:**

- *Experience* → mainly collaborative + critical thinking
- *Analysis (power dynamics)* → mainly systems + collaborative
- *Learning* → mainly normative + critical thinking

**Grade guide:** Excellent in 3+ competencies ≈ top band · Proficient across all four ≈ solid pass · Developing in 2+ ≈ revision needed. Reward concrete, moment-anchored reflection over general statements about negotiation.
