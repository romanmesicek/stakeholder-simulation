# Role Overview & Deal Space

For the facilitator only. Every group's outcome ladder, red lines, and where the deal space actually lies. (Default bachelor setup: the six groups below; Indigenous Community and Technical Expert Panel can be added as optional seventh/eighth groups.)

*ZOPA = Zone of Possible Agreement — the range where the parties' acceptable outcomes overlap, so a deal is possible. Outside it, at least one party is better off walking away (its no-deal outcome).*

## Outcome ladders

| Group | Best | Acceptable | Red line / must avoid |
|-------|------|------------|----------------------|
| **Management** | 7 years, <€2.8 bn | 6–8 years, <€3.1 bn | Budget >€3.5 bn; power failures; mass protests |
| **Workers** | €250–300 M fund, 70% renewable jobs, 7–8 years | €200 M, 50% jobs, 6–7 years | <€100 M support; forced layoffs; timeline <5 years |
| **Community** | €300 M fund, 80% local siting, 10 y tax replacement | €200 M, 65% siting, 7 y | <€100 M; school closures |
| **Environmental** | 4–5 years, €250 M restoration, 800+ MW | 5–6 years, €200 M, 700+ MW | Over 6 years; inadequate cleanup |
| **Government** | 6 years, targets met, re-election | 5–7 years, €500 M public investment | Blackouts; cost >€650 M; social crisis |
| **Investors** | 7 years, 10%+ returns, ESG upgrade | 6–8 years, 8.5%+ returns | Returns <7%; unlimited costs; credit downgrade |

Optional groups: **Indigenous** (best: 30% ownership, €80 M restoration; red line: token ownership <15%) · **Technical Panel** (best: 6–7 years, budget incl. contingency; cannot endorse <5 years — holds the Feasibility Certificate if in play).

Every card carries a **"Your no-deal outcome"** line — the group's BATNA. The shared default: unilateral closure over ~10 years, statutory severance only, no funds, €50 M annual penalties.

## Where the deal space lies

**Timeline.** Environmental wants ≤6 years, Workers ≥6–7, reliability requires ≥5–6. The realistic landing zone is **6–7 years** — a deal at ~6 years with strong restoration or front-loaded worker support satisfies the most constraints.

**Money.** Claims total roughly €550–650 M (Workers 200–300, Community 200–300, restoration 200–250) against: €500 M government fund, €200 M contingency (company must agree), €500 M credit headroom to the €3.5 bn cap. **Not everything is fundable** — students must prioritize; that is the design. The key-facts sheet spells out the sources ("Where can money come from?").

**Watch for false bargains.** Spending the same euro twice (contingency as risk buffer *and* worker fund), timelines below 5 years (blackout risk), restoration promises without a funding source.
