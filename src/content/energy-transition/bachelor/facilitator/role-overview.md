# Role Overview & Deal Space

For the facilitator only. Every group's outcome ladder, red lines, the game numbers, and where the deal space lies. The bachelor level plays with 6, 7 or 8 groups: the default six, plus the Indigenous Community and the Technical Expert Panel if you select them when creating the session. All numbers below are the single source of truth; every bachelor file follows them.

*ZOPA = Zone of Possible Agreement: the range where the parties' acceptable outcomes overlap, so a deal is possible. Outside it, at least one party is better off walking away (its no-deal outcome).*

## Outcome ladders

| Group | Best | Acceptable | Red line / must avoid |
|-------|------|------------|----------------------|
| **Management** | 7 years, company spend ≤€2.8 bn | 6-8 years, ≤€3.1 bn | Spend >€3.5 bn; power failures; mass protests |
| **Workers** | €250-300 M fund, 70% renewable jobs, 7-8 years | €200 M, 50% jobs, 6-7 years | <€100 M support; forced layoffs; timeline <5 years |
| **Community** | €300 M fund, 80% local siting, 10 y tax replacement | €200 M, 65% siting, 7 y | <€100 M; school closures |
| **Environmental** | 4-5 years, €250 M restoration, 800+ MW | 5-6 years, €200 M, 700+ MW | Over 6 years; inadequate cleanup |
| **Government** | 6 years, targets met, public share ≤€500 M | 5-7 years, up to €650 M public money | Blackouts; >€650 M; social crisis |
| **Investors** | 7 years, return 10%+ (spend ≤€2.9 bn) | 6-8 years, return 8.5%+ (spend ≤€3.1 bn) | Return <7% (spend >€3.3 bn); uncapped costs; credit downgrade |
| **Indigenous** *(if in play)* | 30% ownership, €80 M river restoration, 120 jobs, €50 M community fund | 25% ownership, €60 M river restoration, 90 jobs | Token ownership <15%; no cleanup; facilities on sacred sites |
| **Technical Panel** *(if in play)* | 6-7 years, contingency intact | 5-7 years, ≥€100 M contingency left | Cannot certify <5 years, cut storage/grid budget, or contingency <€100 M |

Every card carries a **"Your no-deal outcome"** line, the group's walk-away alternative (BATNA). The shared default: unilateral closure over ~10 years, statutory severance only, no funds, €50 M annual penalties.

## The agreement rule

A **Deal Sheet** becomes the conference outcome if it is signed by **Management**, the **Regional Government**, and **at least half of the other groups** in play. If the Technical Panel plays, the Deal Sheet also needs its **Feasibility Certificate**; the Panel does not count among "the other groups".

| Groups in play | "Other groups" | Signatures needed beyond Management + Government |
|----------------|----------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| 6 (default) | Workers, Community, Environmental, Investors | 2 of 4 |
| 7 with Indigenous | + Indigenous | 3 of 5 |
| 7 with Technical | as in 6 | 2 of 4 + Certificate |
| 8 | Workers, Community, Environmental, Investors, Indigenous | 3 of 5 + Certificate |

Competing Deal Sheets are allowed; only one can meet the rule, because Management and Government can sign only one.

## Where the deal space lies

**Timeline.** Environmental wants ≤6 years, Workers ≥6-7, reliability requires ≥5. The realistic landing zone is **6-7 years**; a deal at ~6 years with strong restoration or front-loaded worker support satisfies the most constraints.

**Money, 6 groups.** At Acceptable level the extra claims are Workers €200 M + Community €200 M + restoration €100 M beyond the planned €100 M = **€500 M**, exactly the government fund. At Best level they reach ~€750 M, which forces the contingency or the credit line. Credit lowers the return (every €100 M = −0.8 points), so Investors push to fund packages publicly or through the contingency, and the Technical Panel (if in play) blocks draining the contingency below €100 M.

**Money, 7-8 groups.** Indigenous adds the ownership discount (25% = €75 M). River restoration (€60-80 M) sits inside Environmental's restoration total, so Environmental and Indigenous can make one restoration demand together. Acceptable claims rise to **~€575 M**, more than the government fund: someone must commit contingency (at most €100 M with the Panel in play) or credit. The squeeze gets tighter with each added group; that is the design.

**Watch for false bargains.**
- Spending the same euro twice (contingency as risk buffer *and* as worker fund)
- Timelines below 5 years (blackout risk, not certifiable)
- Restoration or ownership promises without a funding source
- Government promising more than €650 M in total across all packages

## Group sizes

Each table needs at least 2 people. With 8 groups, plan for 16 or more participants; with fewer, play 6 groups and leave out Indigenous and Technical rather than running one-person tables.
