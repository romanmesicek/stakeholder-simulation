# Environmental Alliance
## Stakeholder Role Card

---

### WHO YOU ARE

You represent environmental organizations fighting for climate action, clean air, and ecosystem protection. You speak for public health, future generations, and the natural world that cannot speak for itself.

**Your authority:** Scientific and health data, legal expertise to challenge permits in court, the ability to mobilize protests and campaigns, and strong media relationships.

**Who you answer to:** Your members and donors, the scientific community whose evidence you use, and the public, who trust you to be credible.

---

### YOUR SITUATION

**Climate Crisis:**
- PowerShift plants emit 4 million tons CO₂ annually (35% of the region's emissions)
- A 5-year instead of a 10-year closure prevents 20 million tons of CO₂
- The law requires a 45% regional emissions cut by 2030; your alliance demands 50%

**Health Impacts:**
- 50 premature deaths annually from coal pollution
- Childhood asthma rates 28% near the plants (vs 18% elsewhere)
- Mercury contamination: 300% above safe levels in local fish

**Restoration:**
- The company's plan contains only about €100 million for site restoration
- Your restoration figure covers the plant sites **and** the river near the Southern Plant (river clean-up: €60-80 million)
- Studies show ecosystems recover quickly with proper cleanup: water quality improves within 2-3 years, fish populations return within 5 years

---

### YOUR GOALS

1. **Fast closure:** Maximum 5-6 years, not 10
2. **Environmental restoration:** Full cleanup of soil and river contamination
3. **Maximum renewables:** At least 800 MW of clean energy

---

### YOUR RESOURCES

**What you control:**
- Scientific evidence and health data
- Legal expertise (can challenge permits)
- Public mobilization (protests, campaigns)
- Media influence
- Moral authority (you represent the public interest)

**What you can offer:**
- Public support for a responsible transition
- Positive media coverage for climate leadership
- Technical expertise on restoration
- No legal challenges if standards are met

---

### YOUR CONSTRAINTS (Non-Negotiable)

- **Maximum 5-6 year timeline:** Climate science demands urgency
- **Full environmental restoration:** Legally binding cleanup commitments with a named budget
- **No compromised standards:** Full compliance with environmental laws

---

### KEY FACTS TO USE

- Every year of coal = 50 preventable deaths from air pollution
- 5-year closure prevents 20 million tons CO₂, about what 4 million cars emit in a year
- Solar and wind are already cheaper than coal (€25-45 vs €65-150 per MWh)
- Children's asthma rates 28% vs 18% baseline

---

### SUCCESS FOR YOU

**Best:** 4-5 year transition. €250 million restoration. 800+ MW renewables.

**Acceptable:** 5-6 years. €200 million restoration. 700+ MW renewables.

**Avoid:** Over 6 years. Inadequate cleanup. Weak enforcement.

**Your no-deal outcome:** Coal burns up to 10 more years (≈40 million extra tons of CO₂) and clean-up stays at the legal minimum.

---

### NEGOTIATION TIPS

- **Speed costs money for others.** A faster closure is easier to accept if workers and communities are protected. Supporting their packages can buy you years.
- **Restoration needs a source.** €200 million means €100 million beyond the company's plan. Name where it comes from.
- **Trade-offs to consider:** a few months more on the timeline vs. a bigger, binding restoration budget; protest pressure vs. a seat in the coalition.
- **Your vulnerability:** Below 5 years the grid is at risk. Push for speed, but a demand that causes blackouts costs you public trust.

---

### USEFUL PHRASES

- "Every year of delay means 50 preventable deaths from air pollution..."
- "Climate science demands action now. This is the critical decade..."
- "We support a just transition: fair worker packages make fast closure fair..."
- "Restored ecosystems create tourism and recreation benefits..."
