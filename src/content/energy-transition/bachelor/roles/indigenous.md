# Indigenous Community
## Stakeholder Role Card

---

### WHO YOU ARE

You represent 3,000 Indigenous community members whose traditional lands surround the Southern Plant. Your community has lived on this land for thousands of years. Your delegation includes elders, the community council, and younger members who want work at home.

**Your authority:** Constitutionally recognized rights, including consultation before any development on your land; land claims covering some of the region's best wind and solar sites; and legal standing to challenge permits in court.

**Who you answer to:** Your community council and elders, your families, and the generations who will inherit this land and river.

---

### YOUR SITUATION

**Historical Harm:**
- The Southern Plant was built in 1990 with minimal consultation
- River polluted: mercury in fish 300% above safe limits
- Traditional fishing no longer safe
- Respiratory illness 23% above the regional average

**Your Community:**
- 90 members work at the Southern Plant (45% of its 200 workers)
- Unemployment: 35% (vs 8% regional)
- Exceptional wind and solar resources on your territory

**What ownership means here:** The company can sell you a share of the facilities built on your territory at a discount, so your community earns from them for decades. Each 10% stake costs about €30 million (the discount). Someone has to pay it: the company, the government fund, or a mix.

---

### YOUR GOALS

1. **River restoration:** Clean water within 7 years, €60-80 million budget
2. **Ownership stake:** At least 25% of the facilities on your land, not just jobs
3. **Jobs for your people:** 90-120 guaranteed positions plus training
4. **Respect your rights:** No development on your land without consultation and consent

---

### YOUR RESOURCES

**What you control:**
- Legal rights: constitutional protections, international frameworks
- Strategic land: some of the best renewable sites are on your territory
- Moral authority: the public supports Indigenous environmental justice

**What you can offer:**
- Land access for renewable development
- Smooth permitting through community support
- Workforce and traditional knowledge of the land and river

---

### YOUR CONSTRAINTS (Non-Negotiable)

- **River must be restored:** Cannot accept any deal without a funded cleanup
- **Real ownership required:** Jobs alone are not enough
- **Sacred sites protected:** No facilities in cultural zones
- **Your consent matters:** No decisions imposed without consultation

---

### KEY FACTS TO USE

- Mercury poisoned our fish, our traditional food source for millennia
- 90 of our members already work at the Southern Plant: we have the skills
- Our territory has some of the best renewable resources in the country
- We are not going anywhere: we will remain on this land

---

### SUCCESS FOR YOU

**Best:** 30% ownership, €80 million river restoration, 120 jobs, €50 million community fund.

**Acceptable:** 25% ownership, €60 million river restoration, 90 jobs, community benefit fund.

**Avoid:** Token ownership (under 15%), inadequate cleanup, jobs without lasting wealth.

**Your no-deal outcome:** Up to 10 more years of pollution on your lands, restoration at the legal minimum, no ownership stake, no consultation precedent.

---

### NEGOTIATION TIPS

- **Your land is your strongest card.** The company wants your sites; a stake in those facilities is the fair price.
- **The river is a shared cause.** River clean-up is part of the restoration the Environmental Alliance demands. One joint restoration demand is stronger than two separate ones.
- **Trade-offs to consider:** a larger ownership stake vs. more guaranteed jobs; an earlier river cleanup vs. a bigger fund later.
- **Your vulnerability:** You are the smallest group, and others may try to settle the big money questions without you. Make sure your items are on the Deal Sheet in writing.

---

### USEFUL PHRASES

- "The river you call a 'cooling system' we call the life-giver..."
- "Development on our territory requires partnership, or it faces legal challenge..."
- "We're not asking for charity. We're offering partnership..."
- "Restoration. Ownership. Employment. Rights. These are our requirements."
