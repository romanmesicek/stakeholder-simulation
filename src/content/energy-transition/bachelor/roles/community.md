# Local Community Coalition
## Stakeholder Role Card

---

### WHO YOU ARE

You represent community leaders, business owners, and residents from Northern Town (15,000 people) and the Southern Region (8,000 people). Your delegation includes the Northern Town mayor, the chamber of commerce, and the school district. The coal plants provide a large share of local jobs and fund schools and services.

**Your authority:** Local councils decide on planning permits for new facilities, and community acceptance (the "social license") decides whether projects run smoothly or face years of opposition. You also have direct lines to regional politicians and the local media.

**Who you answer to:** 23,000 residents, 180 local businesses, and the families whose schools and hospitals depend on plant taxes.

---

### YOUR SITUATION

**Northern Town:**
- Population: 15,000
- Plant provides 40% of local jobs
- Plant taxes fund 55% of schools
- Property values already dropped 15%
- Youth unemployment: 28% (many leaving for cities)

**Southern Region:**
- Population: 8,000 (including the Indigenous community)
- Plant provides 25% of employment
- Good potential for renewable energy development

**The tax gap:** The plants pay €8 million a year in taxes (€5 million municipal + €3 million regional). Without replacement, Northern Town must close schools or raise property taxes by 60%.

---

### YOUR GOALS

1. **Economic diversification:** Break the dependency on one employer
2. **Maintain public services:** Keep schools and hospitals open
3. **Create youth opportunities:** Give young people reasons to stay

---

### YOUR RESOURCES

**What you control:**
- Social license for renewable projects (you can support or oppose)
- Influence over local permits and approvals
- Political connections to regional politicians
- Prime land for wind and solar installations
- Media access to shape public opinion

**What you can offer:**
- Sites for renewable facilities (70-80% of capacity could be built locally)
- Community cooperation and positive media
- Fast-track local permits
- Available workforce and a technical training center

---

### YOUR CONSTRAINTS (Non-Negotiable)

- **Replace the €8 million annual tax revenue** (€5 million municipal + €3 million regional), or schools close
- **Local renewable siting:** At least 65% of the new capacity built locally
- **Economic development fund:** At least €200 million
- **Youth programs:** Training and scholarships for young people

---

### KEY FACTS TO USE

- Plant closure removes ~€45 million a year from the local economy (payroll plus spending), supporting 180 businesses
- €5 million municipal tax loss = school closures or a 60% property tax increase (€3 million more is lost regionally)
- Local siting brings construction jobs, permanent jobs and new tax income
- Germany's coal regions received about €40 billion in structural support

---

### SUCCESS FOR YOU

**Best:** €300 million community fund. 80% of renewables sited locally. Tax replacement for 10 years.

**Acceptable:** €200 million fund. 65% local siting. Tax support for 7 years.

**Avoid:** Under €100 million. Minimal local benefit. School closures.

**Your no-deal outcome:** Slow decline. The tax base erodes over a 10-year unmanaged closure with no fund, no local siting commitments, no support.

---

### NEGOTIATION TIPS

- **Your sites are your currency.** The company needs land and permits; local siting also replaces part of the lost tax income.
- **Separate one-off money from yearly money.** A fund is paid once; the tax gap comes back every year. Ask for both.
- **Trade-offs to consider:** a larger fund vs. a faster timeline; guaranteed tax replacement vs. a flexible development fund; local jobs vs. the cheapest sites.
- **Your vulnerability:** You compete with the Workers for the same public money, and a rival region could offer the company better sites.

---

### USEFUL PHRASES

- "We hosted these plants for 40 years. We deserve to benefit from clean energy too..."
- "Ghost towns help nobody. Community collapse creates lasting opposition..."
- "We have the perfect sites: excellent resources, infrastructure, workforce..."
- "Fair exchange: communities provide land; development provides jobs and taxes..."
