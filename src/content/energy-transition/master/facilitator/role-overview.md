# Role Overview & Deal Space

For the facilitator only. Every group's outcome ladder, red lines, and where the deal space actually lies.

*ZOPA = Zone of Possible Agreement — the range where the parties' acceptable outcomes overlap, so a deal is possible. Outside it, at least one party is better off walking away (its BATNA / no-deal outcome).*

Every card also carries a **"Your no-deal outcome"** line — the group's BATNA. The shared no-deal default: unilateral minimum-compliance closure over ~10 years, statutory severance only, no funds, €50 M annual climate penalties.

## Where the deal space lies

**Timeline.** The deliberate squeeze: Environmental accepts at most 6 years from agreement, Workers want 6–7+, Technical certifies 6–7. The realistic landing zone is **~6–7 years** — Environmental must be bought in with strong restoration and enforcement, or Workers with front-loaded support. A deal at exactly 6 years with binding milestones satisfies the most constraints.

**Money.** Claims total €550–650 M+ (Workers 200–300, Community 200–300, restoration 200–250, Indigenous 60–80) against sources of: €500 M government fund, €200 M contingency (company must agree), credit facility up to the €3.5 bn ceiling, plus reallocation inside the €400 M decommissioning-and-restoration line. **Not everything is fundable** — forcing prioritization is the design. Event card E5 (EU fund, €150 M) eases this late in the game.

**Structural levers.**
- The Technical Panel's **Feasibility Certificate** is required for any final package — veto-adjacent leverage for an otherwise advisory role.
- Government's €650 M cap and the €50 M penalty clock give it urgency but limited depth.
- Indigenous FPIC and constitutional rights make them cheap to include early and expensive to ignore late.
- Investors' rating/ROIC constraints make an expensive-but-stable deal preferable to a cheap-but-contested one — useful framing against pure cost-cutting.

**Watch for false bargains.** Packages that spend the same euro twice (contingency counted both as risk buffer and as worker fund), timelines below 5 years (uncertifiable), and restoration promises with no funding source.
