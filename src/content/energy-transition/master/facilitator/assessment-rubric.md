# Assessment Rubric — Reflection Assignment

For grading the written reflection (1000–1200 words: Experience / Analysis / Coalitions / Theory / Transfer). Four competencies from established sustainability-competence frameworks, three levels each; weight equally unless your course dictates otherwise.

| Competency | Developing | Proficient | Excellent |
|------------|------------|------------|-----------|
| **Systems thinking** | Describes single events in isolation | Connects stakeholders, constraints and outcomes into cause-effect chains | Analyzes the negotiation as a socio-technical system: feedback loops, path dependencies, unintended consequences |
| **Collaborative competence** | Reports own group's actions only | Analyzes coalition dynamics including other groups' perspectives and costs of membership | Reflects on trust-building, information asymmetry and procedural choices — anchored in concrete moments |
| **Normative competence** | States opinions without criteria | Weighs competing values with explicit criteria | Applies justice dimensions (distributional, procedural, recognition) to the outcome and defends a reasoned position |
| **Critical thinking** | Retells events | Questions assumptions — own role's and other groups' claims | Evaluates evidence quality (contested numbers, commissioned studies, salience shifts) and reflects on own biases in role |

**Mapping to the assignment parts:**

- *Experience* → collaborative + critical thinking
- *Analysis (salience shifts)* → systems + critical thinking
- *Coalitions* → collaborative + systems
- *Theory* → normative + systems (accuracy and fit of the applied concepts)
- *Transfer* → normative + critical thinking

**Grade guide:** Excellent in 3+ competencies ≈ top band · Proficient across all four ≈ solid pass · Developing in 2+ ≈ revision needed. In the Theory part, grade the *fit* between concept and observed moment, not the number of concepts cited.
